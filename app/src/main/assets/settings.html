Settings

Accounts

Each account keeps its own login and site data, separate from all others. The number is its place in the account menu; drag the handle to change the order.

More than one account needs a newer “Android System WebView”. Update it through the app store you use (it is a system component, not part of this app).

Appearance

The app and the NextDNS website switch together. A theme chosen inside the website's own account settings wins over this one.

Behaviour

Pinch to zoom
Zoom the page with two fingers. Open pages reload once

A loaded account switches instantly but uses memory. Others load again when you pick them; you stay signed in either way.

Security

App lock
Ask for your fingerprint, face or screen lock when the app opens. Also hides the app in screenshots and recent apps

Data

About & legal

Privacy

This app is a window onto the NextDNS website. It has no servers of its own and its developer receives nothing from it.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking of any kind.
  • It never reads or sends your NextDNS password, settings or logs anywhere. They go only between your phone and NextDNS, exactly as in a browser.

What is stored on this phone

  • Your settings in this app and the names of your accounts.
  • For each account, what the website itself stores, as a browser would: the login cookie, site data and a cache of the site's files. Each account's data is kept apart from the others. “Sign out” and “Remove” in Accounts delete it; “Clear cache” deletes only the cached files.
  • Android's backup includes this app's settings and account names, never logins or site data.

What is sent over the network

  • The pages you open on the NextDNS website.
  • While the app is open, one small request to the NextDNS test address, to show whether this phone uses NextDNS (the icon in the top bar). It carries no account information.
  • Links to other websites open in your browser, not in this app.

How NextDNS handles your data is described in its own privacy policy (Settings → About & legal).

Permissions

Everything this app is allowed to do on your phone. Android grants all three by itself; none of them reaches your files, camera, microphone, location or contacts.

Internet access
Loads the NextDNS website and checks the connection status
View network state
Reads whether Private DNS is switched on, for the status icon in the top bar
Use fingerprint or face unlock
Shows Android's unlock sheet for App lock. The app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN
